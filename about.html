About MDSI Operations SRL

MDSI Operations SRL provides practical operational support for transport and logistics companies, helping maintain continuity, coordination and disciplined execution.

Founded by Marius Iosif, MDSI Operations SRL is based on around 15 years of hands-on operational experience in European transport and logistics.

This experience includes operational coordination, driver follow-up, fleet monitoring and practical support for transport teams, aligned with European working hours.

The approach is based on practical experience across Benelux, France, Italy, Slovakia and Germany, with Romania as the operational base and exposure to European flows and working rhythms.
